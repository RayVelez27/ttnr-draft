The Building

Downtown's Landmark Tower

Rising roughly 435 feet over downtown New Rochelle, Trump Plaza was built by Cappelli Enterprises between 2005 and 2007 at a reported cost of about $250 million. When it opened it was the tallest building in Westchester County, and it held that title until the twin-towered Ritz-Carlton in White Plains was completed.

The tower sits on the two-acre "Parcel 1A" site, which the city had tried to redevelop for more than 30 years. It was part of the larger downtown revival that began with New Roc City in 1999. Two floors of retail form the base, and an enclosed pedestrian bridge links them to New Roc City.

Life Around 175 Huguenot

The Neighborhood

Getting Around

Steps from Metro-North

New Rochelle station is a short walk away, with trains to Grand Central in about half an hour. LaGuardia is roughly a 26-minute drive.

Shopping & Entertainment

New Roc City Next Door

Movies, dining and recreation are a minute's walk across the skybridge, and downtown shops and banks are nearby.

Parks & Waterfront

Close to the Sound

Davenport Neck Bird Sanctuary, Sheldrake Environmental Center and Ward Acres are among the green spaces within a few miles.
